## Goals

- Test the hardware components individually and identify integration blockers
- Confirm that the Raspberry Pi can be used through a mobile hotspot
- Continue refining the calculations for the Final Design Presentation (FDP) and Final Design Report (FDR)

## What we did

- Completed individual testing of several hardware components
- Confirmed that the Raspberry Pi can connect to a mobile hotspot, allowing the system to be used away from campus
- Tested the motors and completed the calculations needed to select an appropriate power supply
- Began prototyping the housing for the camera and Raspberry Pi
- Identified that the FDP calculations need numerical evidence to support the design decisions

## Individual contributions

- **Hannah Lamarche:** Tested the Raspberry Pi connection through a mobile hotspot to support portable operation
- **Dennis Lee:** Tested the motors with Trinity and completed power-supply calculations
- **Trinity Hsu:** Tested the motors with Dennis and contributed to the power-supply calculations
- **Leo You:** Began prototyping the camera and Raspberry Pi housing

## Decisions / learnings

- Mobile-hotspot connectivity supports using the system in locations without campus Wi-Fi
- Motor testing and power-supply calculations will inform the final power-system selection
- Numerical evidence must be added to all FDP calculations so that the design decisions can be evaluated and reproduced

## Blockers

- The team does not yet have its own power supply (on its way now)
- Access to on-campus facilities is limited by facility hours

## Next

- Perform a connectivity test with all hardware connected
- Continue refining the FDP and FDR calculations
- Add numerical evidence to support every FDP calculation
- Start on CAD for P0 testing soon
