## Goals

- Define the design problem for the project proposal
- Identify the need the project will address
- Clarify the main objectives, constraints, and criteria
- Begin shaping the scope of the final design

## What we did

- Worked on the project proposal by stating the design problem and the need it addresses
- Discussed the main objectives the design should achieve, including the expected function and overall purpose
- Identified key constraints that will affect the design, such as size, cost, time, and available materials
- Started listing criteria that will be used to evaluate whether the final design is successful
- Used the proposal discussion to narrow the project scope and align the team on what the system should do

## Decisions / learnings

- A strong proposal needs a clear design problem before detailed design work can begin
- Needs, objectives, constraints, and criteria should be written early so the team can stay aligned
- The proposal helped establish a clearer direction for future subsystem planning
