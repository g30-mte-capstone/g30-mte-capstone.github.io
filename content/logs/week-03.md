## Goals

- Lay out all systems to divide research and design calculations

## What we did

- Broke the project into major subsystems for clearer ownership
- Assigned a primary point of contact for each subsystem
- Added helper roles so each area has backup support and review
- Started outlining the overall schedule and discussed using a Gantt chart
- Began formalizing the project needs, objectives, and constraints

## Decisions / learnings

- Dividing work by subsystem should make research and calculations easier to manage
- Each area needs a clear leader/champion to keep communication organized
- A Gantt chart may be useful for visualizing the timeline and dependencies
- Defining requirements early will help guide design decisions
